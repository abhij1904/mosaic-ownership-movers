What this is

The quantity each company actually sold — subscribers, premium collected — counted and published monthly by its regulator or industry council, independent of the company's own accounts and weeks ahead of the quarter being filed. Listed companies link to their terminal page; unlisted entities are kept because market share only means anything against the full reported market. Telecom comes from TRAI's monthly subscription report; insurance premium from the General Insurance Council's flash figures — the industry body whose numbers IRDAI republishes. Every table cell traces to a named public document.
