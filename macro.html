Method & scope

Built from company_operating_metric (ingest/46) — the physical volumes each sector regulator publishes monthly. The industry total is the sum of every entity the regulator reports, reconciled against the source's own grand total where it prints one. Insurance covers every licensed insurer (GI Council + Life Council); telecom covers TRAI's tabulated operators. Auto (VAHAN per-maker) and further Thurro/Data-Prism sectors land in this same shape as their collectors ship — each will show the industry total alongside the listed players inside it.
